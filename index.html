Four skills, one at a time. Tap things, watch what happens.

What do the letters mean?

A letter is just a number we don't know yet. So 3x means 3 lots of x, or 3 × x. We never bother writing the × between a number and a letter.

Tap each term to see its sign

The sign in front of a term belongs to it. Tap every piece of this expression.

Three words you'll see

Term: one piece of an expression. 4x, 7y and −2 are the terms in 4x + 7y − 2.

Coefficient: the number in front of the letter. In 4x it's 4. A letter on its own, like y, is really 1y.

Power: the small number up top. x³ means x × x × x. A letter on its own is x¹.

Collecting like terms

You can only add or subtract terms that are alike. Think of x as apples and y as bananas. 3 apples + 2 apples = 5 apples, but 3 apples + 2 bananas just stays as it is.

Sort the terms, then collect

Tap a term to send it to its bucket. Letters go together, plain numbers go together. The sign travels with it.

Two traps

x² and x are not like terms. Keep them apart, like apples and apple pies.

Adding doesn't change the power. d² + d² = 2d², not 2d⁴. You've got two of the same thing, that's all.

Expanding brackets

"Expand" and "multiply out" mean the same thing. The number outside the bracket multiplies everything inside, one term at a time.

The minus trap

If there's a minus in front of the bracket, it multiplies everything inside too. Minus × minus = plus. So 18 − 5(2t − 6) gives −10t + 30, not − 30. Highlight both minus signs before you start.

No number in front? 10 − (3w + 4) means −1 lots of the bracket: 10 − 3w − 4.

Factorising

This is expanding in reverse. Find the biggest number that goes into every term, put it outside, and divide each term by it to fill the bracket.

Find the biggest factor

Which is the biggest number that goes into both?

Check it

Expand your answer back out. If you get the original, it's right. And if the bits inside the bracket still share a number, you haven't gone big enough.

Don't lose the 1: 9y + 3 = 3(3y + 1). A bracket can't be empty.
